      About Email Template Builder

      A free visual editor for HTML emails.

      Build newsletters, announcements and campaigns from blocks, preview them on desktop and mobile, then export clean, email-ready HTML. It’s made for marketers, founders and developers who want a good-looking email without writing table markup.

      Privacy

      Your projects (blocks, settings and image links) are saved only in this browser’s local storage on this device. There are no accounts, and the content of your emails is never collected or uploaded. Images are loaded straight from the web addresses you enter.

      This site uses a self-hosted Umami instance for aggregate, cookieless page-view statistics only. If the optional “Send test” feature is switched on, the email you choose to test is passed to the email provider (Resend) to deliver it; this app does not store it.
